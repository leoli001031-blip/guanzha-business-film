# 14｜关键帧提示词

这份文件给 r8 分镜挑了 24 个关键帧，每个带中文画面描述、英文生图提示词、负面提示词、要附的参考图和注意事项。提示词按"干净哑光 3D"写，每条另给一行换成平面风时的替换词。先用它们做定场图和风格测试，确认后再出动态。文末的"角色一致性写法"是固定描述，所有关键帧原样复用，不要每次重写。

使用方式：把文末对应角色的固定描述贴在提示词最前面，再接镜头提示词；参考图按列出的路径一起喂给模型（能传图的模型优先传角色锚定图）。所有画面都不要生成文字，字幕、信息卡、标识在后期叠。

通用风格词（3D 版）：`clean matte 3D render, soft rounded forms, matte non-glossy materials, no transparency, no chrome, soft studio lighting with gentle ambient occlusion, simple uncluttered background, 16:9, cinematic framing, no text, no watermark`

通用风格词（平面版替换）：`flat vector illustration, thick clean black outlines, flat fill colors, minimal cel shading, sticker-like, simple shapes, matching the official character sheet style, 16:9, no text`

通用负面提示词：`glossy, glass, transparent, chrome, oily highlights, lens flare, photorealistic human, extra limbs, extra fingers, deformed face, asymmetric eyes, text, letters, watermark, logo, signature, busy background, clutter, blurry`

## 角色一致性的提示词写法

每个角色一段固定描述，所有关键帧原样复用，不改词序。先写角色再写镜头，模型对前面的词权重更高。

### 猹猹（主角）

`Cha: a small chubby round white creature mascot, pure matte white body shaped like a soft ball with a slightly larger head, two tiny rounded ears on top, tiny round black bead eyes set wide, a small black button nose, a small simple smiling mouth, no cheek blush, short stubby arms ending in small round paw-hands, short stubby legs, a big rounded white tail with two short dark dash marks, wearing a plain bright green short-sleeve T-shirt, cute and simple, consistent proportions`

### 猹妹

`Cha-mei: a small chibi girl character with a brown bear-ear hooded hat, two small round bear ears on the hood, straight brown bangs and brown side hair with two small low pigtails peeking out, a round pale face, pink round blush on both cheeks, tiny round black bead eyes, a tiny nose, a gentle small smile, small round hands, wearing a matching brown hooded top; below the waist: the same brown as a short one-piece with short stubby legs and small round feet (full-body design pending), same head-to-body ratio as the white creature, cute and simple, consistent proportions`

下半身是提案，等全身设定定稿后把括号里那句换成定稿描述。

### 商务猹

`Business Cha: the same small round white creature as Cha (same face, ears, paws, tail) but wearing a fitted black suit jacket, white shirt, black tie, and black rounded sunglasses, calm composed posture, slight corporate gravitas, no green T-shirt`

楼顶两只和小队用同一段，加 `two of them standing side by side, identical` 或 `a team of five identical`。

### D

`D: the capital letter D taken from the TokenDance wordmark, a bold geometric sans-serif D in matte black, its inner counter containing two short parallel diagonal slashes that read like a pair of eyes, no other facial features, no limbs, no mouth, treated as a living character through squash-and-stretch only, about the same height as the white creature, flat matte black with a faint teal glow at the base when it lands`

### 开发者猹

`Developer Cha: the same small round white creature as Cha but wearing round thin-rimmed glasses and a grey hoodie instead of the green T-shirt, slightly tired but earnest expression`

### 博主猹

`Creator Cha: the same small round white creature as Cha wearing large over-ear headphones around his ears, green T-shirt, expressive and a bit dramatic`

### 一致性的三条规则

1. 同一镜头里出现几个角色，就把几段固定描述按出场重要性依次贴上，不省略。
2. 不要在镜头提示词里重复描述外观（例如再写一次"white creature with green shirt"），只写动作、表情、构图和光。
3. 换画风时只换文末的风格词，不改角色描述；平面风下在角色描述末尾加 `thick clean black outline`。
